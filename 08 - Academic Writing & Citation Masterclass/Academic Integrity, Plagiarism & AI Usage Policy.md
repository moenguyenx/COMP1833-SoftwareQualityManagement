## 🚫 Understanding University of Greenwich Plagiarism Regulations

At Level 7 (Master's level), the University of Greenwich enforces strict penalties for academic misconduct, ranging from zero marks on the assessment to formal disciplinary committee hearings:

### 1. Types of Academic Misconduct to Guard Against
- **Direct Plagiarism**: Copying text verbatim from published papers, textbooks, online articles, or student repositories without enclosing in quotation marks and providing full citations.
- **Collusion**: Sharing individual section drafts (Parts 2, 4, 5) with team members such that individual work is cloned or shared.
- **Contract Cheating**: Paying third-party essay mills or commissioning external individuals to write sections.
- **Undisclosed AI Generation**: Pasting raw outputs from large language models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) directly into your submission document without attribution.

## 🤖 Responsible & Lawful Use of AI in COMP1833

The course policy allows AI tools to be used as cognitive scaffolding, provided transparency and attribution guidelines are strictly observed:

### ✅ Permissible Uses of AI:
- **Ideation & Brainstorming**: Exploring candidate architectures, brainstorming potential failure modes, or identifying applicable ISO standards.
- **Structure & Outlining**: Using Obsidian templates and frameworks to organize your thoughts and ensure complete coverage of marking rubrics.
- **Proofreading & Syntax Checking**: Checking grammatical flow, spelling, and LaTeX/Mermaid syntax.

### ❌ Prohibited Uses of AI (Academic Misconduct):
- **Raw Copy-Pasting**: Prompting an AI to *"write my Part 3 Software Quality Plan"* and submitting the generated paragraphs as your own original analysis.
- **Fabricated Citations ("Hallucinations")**: Submitting non-existent journal papers or false standard clause numbers generated by AI.

## 📝 How to Cite AI Content if Directly Quoted

If you directly quote a sentence or definition generated by an AI application, you **MUST** format it in quotation marks and cite it according to University of Greenwich / Cite Them Right standards:

### In-Text Citation Format:
> As generated by OpenAI’s ChatGPT model, *"Software configuration management establishes an auditable baseline..."* (OpenAI, 2026).

### Reference List Format (Cite Them Right Harvard):
- OpenAI (2026) *ChatGPT* (Model: GPT-4o) [Large language model]. Available at: https://chat.openai.com/ (Accessed: 24 October 2026).

└────────────┬────────────┘
             ▼
