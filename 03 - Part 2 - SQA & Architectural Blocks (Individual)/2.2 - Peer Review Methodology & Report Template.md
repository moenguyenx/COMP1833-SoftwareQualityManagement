## 🔍 The IEEE 1028 Formal Inspection Framework

```
                             IEEE 1028 SOFTWARE INSPECTION LIFECYCLE
┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
│ 1. Planning  │ ──► │ 2. Overview  │ ──► │3. Preparation│ ──► │4. Inspection │ ──► │  5. Rework   │
│ & Role Assign│     │ Presentation │     │ (Indiv Check)│     │ Logging Mtg  │     │ & Verification
└──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘
```

1. **Planning**: Reviewer and author schedule the inspection and establish objective defect checklists.
2. **Overview**: Author provides a brief architectural walkthrough of the two functionalities.
3. **Preparation**: Reviewer independently inspects the diagrams and data structures against quality checklists.
4. **Inspection Meeting**: Reviewer logs discovered defects without discussing blame; anomalies are formally classified.
5. **Rework & Follow-up**: Author implements fixes in Section 2.1; reviewer verifies and signs off on closure.

### 4.0 Qualitative Evaluation & Critical Discussion (Level 7 Quality)

The architectural blocks presented by the author demonstrate commendable modularity and adhere to modern microservice engineering principles:
1. **Strengths**:
   - The separation of concern between the *Ingestion Gateway* and the *Predictive ML Classifier* ensures that computational spikes during inference do not degrade the ingestion throughput of emergency calls.
   - The selection of TimescaleDB for time-series facility telemetry is technically sound and aligns with industry best practices for high-write-volume IoT telemetry.
2. **Critical Areas for Enhancement**:
   - While the original design adequately mapped out happy-path workflows, it initially exhibited naive assumptions regarding data hygiene. In safety-critical healthcare software, schema validation is the first line of defense against clinical software catastrophe (Pressman and Maxim, 2020). Implementing strict boundary constraints on patient vitals resolves this concern.
   - The decoupling achieved by the Kafka event bus directly fulfills the coursework mandate to avoid single points of failure, ensuring that downstream failures in the notification subsystem do not halt bed allocation.
