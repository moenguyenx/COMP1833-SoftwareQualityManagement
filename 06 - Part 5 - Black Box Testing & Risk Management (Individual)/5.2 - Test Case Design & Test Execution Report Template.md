## 🔍 Critical Analysis of Testing Results (Bridge to Part 5.2)

The execution of these 8 black box test cases revealed two critical vulnerabilities in the software under test:
1. **Defect in Triage Bed Allocator (`TC-F1-04`)**:
   - When the maximum systolic blood pressure boundary (`300 mmHg`) was supplied, the bed allocation algorithm failed with an unhandled exception rather than smoothly allocating an emergency resuscitation bed. In a live hospital, this unhandled crash would freeze the triage terminal, delaying immediate life-saving care.
2. **Defect in High-Concurrency Telemetry Ingestion (`TC-F2-04`)**:
   - Under bursts exceeding 10,000 MQTT packets/sec, the database connection pool became saturated, causing an 8.5% packet drop. This means critical warning signals from failing hospital equipment could be permanently lost during network spikes.

These two identified failures serve as the direct empirical foundation for the **Two Product Risks and Controls** in [[06 - Part 5 - Black Box Testing & Risk Management (Individual)/5.4 - Risk Register & Control Implementation Template|Part 5.2]].
